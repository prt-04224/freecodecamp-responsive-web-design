Tiramisu

Tiramisu is a classic, no-bake Italian dessert that creates a rich and sophisticated treat using only five staple ingredients. By layering coffee-soaked biscuits with a velvety cream mixture, you get a perfect balance of bitter coffee and sweet dairy without ever turning on the oven.

Ingredients

  • Biscuits: 24 ladyfingers (Savoiardi)
  • Coffee: 1 cup strong brewed espresso, cooled
  • Dairy: 250 grams Mascarpone cheese
  • Eggs: 3 large eggs (separated into yolks and whites)
  • Sugar: ¼ cup granulated sugar

Instructions

  1. Separate the egg yolks from the whites into two clean bowls
  2. Add the sugar to the egg yolks and whisk until pale and thick.
  3. Fold the Mascarpone cheese gently into the yolk mixture until smooth.
  4. Whip the egg whites in the second bowl until stiff peaks form.
  5. Fold the whipped whites gently into the Mascarpone mixture using a spatula.
  6. Pour the cooled, strong espresso into a shallow bowl.
  7. Dip each ladyfinger biscuit quickly into the coffee for 1–2 seconds.
  8. Avoid over-soaking the biscuits to keep them from getting soggy.
  9. Arrange a single layer of coffee-soaked ladyfingers in an 8x8 inch dish.
  10. Spread half of the Mascarpone cream evenly over the biscuit layer.
  11. Repeat with a second layer of soaked ladyfingers on top.
  12. Spread the remaining Mascarpone cream evenly over the top layer.
  13. Cover the dish tightly with plastic wrap or a lid.
  14. Refrigerate for at least 4 to 6 hours before slicing.
  15. Dust the top layer with cocoa powder right before serving.
