Discover Mechuka!

Mechuka offers an extraordinary escape for travelers seeking pristine landscapes, rich tribal culture, and untouched wilderness. Nestled in a secluded valley near the Indo-China border, this hidden gem invites adventure seekers and peace-lovers alike to explore its lush meadows, pine forests, and dramatic mountain backdrops. Visitors can immerse themselves in the distinct heritage of the Memba people, trek through vibrant rhododendron trails, or experience thrilling outdoor activities like river rafting, paragliding, and camping under star-filled skies. Whether you want to witness centuries-old Buddhist traditions or simply watch wild horses graze along the turquoise Siyom River, Mechuka delivers a rare, authentic journey far off the beaten path.

Packages

Travel operators offer diverse curated packages to Mechuka tailored by duration, activity level, and region, with standard 5-to-6-day sightseeing packages starting from Dibrugarh generally priced around ₹19,600 to ₹37,800 per person depending on group size. For deep immersion, travelers can choose 7-to-9-day cultural itineraries that introduce the unique lifestyle of the local Galo and Memba tribes, or rigorous multi-day trekking expeditions exploring high-altitude lakes like Pasang Sonam Tso. Adventure agencies also craft extensive 11-day mega-expeditions that bundle Mechuka with other remote regions like Anini, featuring all-inclusive private SUV transit, homestay accommodations, and mandatory Inner Line Permits (ILP).
