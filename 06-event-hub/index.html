LA 2028 Summer Olympics

2028 Summer Olympics, officially known as LA28, will take place in Los Angeles, California, from July 14 to July 30, 2028. This international event marks the third time Los Angeles will host the Summer Games, following historic events in 1932 and 1984. The upcoming Games uniquely rely on existing and temporary venues.

Date: July 14, 2028 -- July 30, 2028

Asian Games 2026

Asian Games, also known as the Asiad, is a major continental multi-sport event held every four years for athletes across Asia. Recognized as the second-largest multi-sport competition globally after the Olympic Games, it was first hosted in New Delhi, India, in March 1951. The event promotes regional unity, friendship, and athletic excellence through a diverse range of sports.

Date: September 19, 2026 -- October 4, 2026

Past Events

FIFA World Cup 2026

FIFA World Cup is a global football tournament held every four years by FIFA. National teams from all over the world play intense qualification matches for three years to reach the final stage. The main event brings 48 top teams together to compete in host countries for about a month. It is the most watched sports event on earth, uniting billions of fans. Brazil holds the record for the most titles with five wins. The championship creates lasting national pride and unforgettable athletic moments.

ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026

The ICC Men's T20 World Cup is a major global cricket tournament played every two years. Teams compete in the fast Twenty20 format where each side plays just 20 overs. India is the most successful team with three titles, winning the latest 2026 event at home. Matches feature intense group stages and knockout rounds. The event brings together top international squads from around the world. It showcases explosive batting and tight bowling skills.
