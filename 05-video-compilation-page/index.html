Fifa World Cup

The FIFA World Cup is the biggest and most watched sports event on earth. Teams from around the globe play every four years to win the world championship trophy. Nations fight through years of tough qualifying games just to get a spot. Fans unite across borders to support their home countries. The games fill stadiums with loud cheers and pure excitement. It remains the ultimate test of skill and national pride in global sport.

The New Format

48 teams split into 12 groups of four, playing 104 total matches. The top two teams in each group plus the eight best third-place finishers advance to an added Round of 32 knockout phase.

Sunflower

Released in October 2018 by American artists Post Malone and Swae Lee, "Sunflower" is a dreamy, upbeat pop and hip-hop track written for the animated film Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse. The song became a massive global hit, known for its smooth vocal blend and emotional lyrics about a rocky, unstable relationship.

Die For u

Released on November 22, 2021, "Die For You" by VALORANT and Grabbitz serves as the official anthem for the inaugural VALORANT Champions tournament. Fusing energetic rock and electronic elements, the song captures the intense pressure, sacrifice, and ultimate dedication required of competitors on the world stage.
